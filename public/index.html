Free · runs in your browser + a tiny server

Turn your handwriting
into a real font.

Print the template, fill it in with a pen, take a photo. We find the corner markers, straighten the page, trace every letter into smooth curves, and hand you a .ttf you can install anywhere.

1

Print the template

Two A4 pages covering all 94 printable ASCII characters. Page 1 alone (capitals, digits, basic punctuation) is enough to get started.

Page 1 · A–Z 0–9 . , ! ? Page 2 · a–z @ # $ % …
Download PDF template
  • Print at any size; the corner squares handle scaling.
  • Use a black felt-tip or gel pen. Pencil is too faint.
  • Sit letters on the blue baseline; g j p q y hang below it.
  • Leave a box empty to skip that character.
2

Upload photos or scans

Shoot from straight above in even light, with all four black corner squares visible. Pages are recognised automatically, in any order.

    How to install the font
    • Windows: right-click the .ttf → Install.
    • macOS: double-click the .ttf → Install Font.
    • iPhone / iPad: use a font installer app, or upload to apps that accept custom fonts.
    • Web: @font-face { font-family: "My Hand"; src: url(myhand.ttf); }
    • Regenerating with the same name? Uninstall the old one first, or give it a new name.
    Made with OpenCV, potrace and fontTools. Your photos are processed once and never stored.
